YOUR EXPEDITION GUIDE

How to play

Read the flag, trust your knowledge, and see how far your passport can take you.

  1. 01

    Study the flag

    A flag appears. Look closely at its colors, symbols, and pattern.

  2. 02

    Choose a country

    Pick the right answer from the four country options.

  3. 03

    Build your score

    Correct guesses earn a point and add that country to your exploration passport.

  4. 04

    Watch your lives

    You have five lives. When they run out, the expedition pauses for two minutes. Your passport stamps are kept.

  5. 05

    Unlock new continents

    Complete every country in a continent to unlock the next one.
